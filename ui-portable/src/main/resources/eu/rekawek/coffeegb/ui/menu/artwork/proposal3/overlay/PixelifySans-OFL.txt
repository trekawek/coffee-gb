Pixelify Sans bitmap atlas provenance and license
=================================================

The packaged pixelify-sans-medium-atlas.png and
pixelify-sans-semibold-atlas.png files were rasterized from the approved
build-time font source PixelifySans.ttf. pixelify-sans-display-atlas.png is a
nearest-neighbour 75% horizontal derivative of the SemiBold atlas for the
mockup's tall condensed menu labels.

Source SHA-256:
9ba86cd010a4de309d263ceff8e8044092c9db7efda869620cb9ff1c4389e8a5

The source TTF is intentionally not packaged in ui-portable. Both RGBA atlases
contain the portable compositor's fixed glyph alphabet.

Medium atlas: 384x144
Cell: 24x36; glyph advance: 17; space advance: 10
SHA-256: c0003e3e71ab58fd1ce79f18cac1b21edb2a812fbeff2c5b3badef0129c42a0d

SemiBold atlas: 512x192
Cell: 32x48; glyph advance: 29; space advance: 17
SHA-256: 435074e7fffede8ca887333518a49ab944de9677aff135342f5a1d6dc47f6361

Display atlas: 384x192
Cell: 24x48; glyph advance: 18; space advance: 11
SHA-256: 1d95c6029f34c5ab957b4987cb33263e571a4fbb9f770ef8494807b151e5c689
