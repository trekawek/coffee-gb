Pixelify Sans bitmap atlas provenance and license
=================================================

The packaged pixelify-sans-medium-atlas.png and
pixelify-sans-semibold-atlas.png files were rasterized from the approved
build-time Pixelify Sans Regular, Medium, and SemiBold sources.
pixelify-sans-display-atlas.png is a nearest-neighbour 75% horizontal
derivative of the SemiBold atlas for the mockup's tall condensed menu labels.
pixelify-sans-small-atlas.png is a nearest-neighbour 61% horizontal
derivative of the 36 px Regular atlas for dense rows without changing family
or cap height.
pixelify-sans-notice-atlas.png is a nearest-neighbour 78% horizontal
derivative of the 36 px Medium atlas for the About notice rows. It preserves
the mockup's icon gap and right margin while remaining visibly wider than the
dense Small role.
All roles use the same Pixelify Sans glyph design and never invoke a platform
font renderer. The deterministic build recipe is
tools/generate_pixelify_atlases.py.

Source SHA-256:
9ba86cd010a4de309d263ceff8e8044092c9db7efda869620cb9ff1c4389e8a5

The source TTF is intentionally not packaged in ui-portable. Both RGBA atlases
contain the portable compositor's fixed glyph alphabet.
